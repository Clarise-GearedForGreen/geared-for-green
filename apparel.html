How it’s made

Bottles in, performance apparel out

Every piece begins as a plastic bottle collected through our recycling network. Bottles are sorted, cleaned, and processed into raw material, then spun into performance fiber and woven into fabric.

This collect–verify–recycle–remake–deploy process is documented at every stage, so each garment can be traced back to the bottles that made it.

What’s Your Number?

Every piece tells you exactly what it’s made of
