Champions for GREEN Events

Turn your event into a closed loop

We partner with event organizers and venues nationwide — golf tournaments, conferences, festivals — to collect waste on-site, recycle it through a verified Chain of Custody, and put it back into attendees’ hands as something they’ll actually keep.

What we do

Sustainability that happens where your event actually is

Most event sustainability efforts end at a recycling bin. We track what goes in the bin, make sure it actually gets recycled, and close the loop by turning it into new product — so the impact is provable, not assumed.

  • On-site collection built into your event footprint, not bolted on afterward.
  • Chain of Custody tracking, so reclaimed material is verified from pickup to remanufacture.
  • Recycled material remade into performance apparel and event merchandise.
  • Attendee and stakeholder education, so the sustainability story is visible, not just internal.
  • Proven with recurring partners including golf clubs and tournaments run year over year.
