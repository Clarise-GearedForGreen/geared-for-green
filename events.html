Champions for GREEN Events

Turn your event into a closed loop

We handle waste collection at your event or venue from start to finish. No bins to source, no sort stations to staff, no haulers to coordinate, no guesswork on where it ends up. Every load moves through a verified Chain of Custody and comes out the other side as new products, not the landfill.

What we do

Sustainability that happens where your event actually is

Most event and venue sustainability efforts fail before the material ever leaves the site — wrong bins, no haulers, contamination that gets a whole load rejected at the recycling facility. We handle all of it: we bring the haulers, place the bins, and build the sorting infrastructure on-site, so what goes in actually gets recycled instead of quietly landfilled.

  • Custom-designed bins, placed and staffed on-site — built to make sorting intuitive for guests and vendors, so streams stay clean and no load gets rejected at the recycling facility before it’s ever processed.
  • Pickup logistics, handled end to end — we supply and coordinate the haulers ourselves, so you’re not sourcing a vendor or chasing a pickup window. Routes are scheduled around your event or venue’s timeline, with pickups scheduled to handle multi-day events or large venues, so bins never sit full or overflow.
  • Chain of Custody tracking — tracked diligently by our team and verified by Intertek, so reclaimed material is accounted for from pickup to remanufacture. Events and venues can also tap into technologies like TRACE AI to reward guests and incentivize recycling on-site, and RegenerateX™ to support Scope 3, EPR, and ESG compliance reporting.
  • Visible impact, not a footnote — the sustainability story doesn’t stay buried in a report. We package results for attendees on-site and stakeholders after the fact, so the effort your event or venue put into recycling actually gets seen, not just logged.
  • Waste, remade into wearable and usable proof — reclaimed material is converted into performance apparel and event merchandise, with each apparel piece tagged to show exactly how many recycled bottles went into it, as well as operational products like trash bags, trash bins, and other plastic goods used to run your next event — closing the loop back into your own operations.
