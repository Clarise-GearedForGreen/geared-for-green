What we bring

Infrastructure and education, not just a recycling bin

Campus & Community for GREEN pairs physical Chain of Custody recycling infrastructure with a curriculum that teaches students and staff how a circular economy actually works — so the program outlasts any one graduating class.

  • On-campus collection points tied into the same Chain of Custody tracking used across Champions for GREEN.
  • Reclaimed material remade into campus-branded apparel and everyday products.
  • A four-pillar sustainability framework, tailored to your campus or community.
  • Education built in, so students understand the economics of recycling, not just the mechanics.
