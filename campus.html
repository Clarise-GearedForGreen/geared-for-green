Campus & Community for GREEN

Turn campus waste into a lesson plan — and a line item saved.

Our program delivers economic, environmental, and social value by rethinking waste as a sustainable raw material, ready to be transformed into new products. Implementing it turns your campus into a model for how waste should be handled — avoiding landfill, saving money, and educating tomorrow’s sustainable leaders.

What we bring

Infrastructure and education, not just a recycling bin

Campus & Community for GREEN pairs physical Chain of Custody recycling infrastructure with a curriculum that teaches students and staff how a circular economy actually works — so the program outlasts any one graduating class.

Collection points built into daily campus life

On-site infrastructure placed where waste actually happens — dorms, dining halls, athletics facilities, and campus events — so recycling is part of the routine, not a special project.

Chain of Custody tracking, verified by Intertek

Every batch is tracked from pickup through remanufacture. Your sustainability office gets documented, audited numbers to report on — not estimates.

TRACE AI, turning recycling into an incentive

Students earn rewards for participating, so recycling becomes something they engage with, not a chore they ignore.

RegenerateX™, built for institutional reporting

Support Scope 3, EPR, and ESG reporting — the kind of data campuses already track toward sustainability plans and ratings — without building the infrastructure yourself.

Remade into campus-branded product, not landfill

Reclaimed material comes back as campus-branded apparel and everyday products, closing the loop back into your own operations while cutting disposal costs.
