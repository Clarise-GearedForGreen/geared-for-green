How it works

The Chain of Custody

One process, tracked end to end, applied across all three brands. Nothing gets lost between collection and the finished product in someone’s hands.

01

Collect

Reclaim plastic bottles and materials at the source — on-site at events, on campus, or across a corporate supply chain.

02

Verify

Track every batch through the eco-supply chain so what goes in is provably what comes out.

03

Recycle

Break reclaimed material down and spin it into sustainable, performance-grade yarns.

04

Remake

Manufacture apparel and products built to hold up — not single-use sustainability.

05

Deploy

Deliver the finished product back to the people and places that started the loop.
